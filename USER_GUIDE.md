
# Kenyan Safari Resort - User Guide

Welcome to the Kenyan Safari Resort booking platform! This guide will help you navigate through our application and make the most of your safari planning experience.

## Getting Started

1. **Create an Account**: Click on the "Sign Up" button in the navigation bar to create your personal account.
2. **Browse Experiences**: Explore our accommodations and safari activities without logging in.
3. **Make a Booking**: Sign in to reserve your lodge and activities.

## Safari Guidelines

For the best safari experience:
- Wear neutral-colored clothing (avoid bright colors)
- Bring sunscreen, hat, and insect repellent
- Follow all guide instructions at all times
- Maintain a respectful distance from wildlife
- Keep noise levels to a minimum
- Respect local Maasai customs and traditions

## Wildlife Viewing

The Maasai Mara and surrounding reserves offer some of the world's best wildlife viewing:

- **The Big Five**: Lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhinoceros
- **Great Migration**: Witness millions of wildebeest and zebra (July to October)
- **Big Cats**: Experience some of Africa's highest concentrations of lions and cheetahs
- **Bird Life**: Over 500 bird species throughout the region

## Booking Policies

- **Reservations**: Bookings require a 20% deposit
- **Cancellation**: Full refund if cancelled 48 hours before check-in
- **Children**: Special rates available for children under 12
- **Groups**: Discounts available for groups of 6 or more
- **Park Fees**: Conservation fees for park entry are included in all bookings

## Preparing for Your Safari

- **Vaccinations**: Please consult your doctor about recommended vaccinations for Kenya
- **Visa**: Most visitors require a visa to enter Kenya (available online or on arrival)
- **Currency**: The Kenyan Shilling (KES) is the local currency
- **Weather**: Kenya has two rainy seasons: March-May and November-December
- **Packing**: Light clothing, sturdy shoes, camera with zoom lens, binoculars
