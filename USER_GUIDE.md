
# Kenyan Safari Resort - User Guide

Welcome to the Kenyan Safari Resort booking platform! This guide will help you navigate through our application and make the most of your safari planning experience.

## Getting Started

1. **Create an Account**: Click on the "Sign Up" button in the navigation bar to create your personal account.
2. **Browse Experiences**: Explore our accommodations and safari activities without logging in.
3. **Make a Booking**: Sign in to reserve your room and activities.

## Features Overview

### Home Page
- Featured rooms and special offers
- Quick search functionality
- Featured safari activities
- Testimonials from previous guests

### Accommodation
- Browse through our range of luxury accommodations
- Filter by capacity, amenities, and price
- View detailed room information and photos
- Check availability for your desired dates

### Safari Activities
- Explore our range of safari experiences
- Filter by difficulty level, duration, and type
- Read detailed descriptions of each activity
- View ratings from previous guests

### Booking Process
1. Select your check-in and check-out dates
2. Choose your room type
3. Add safari activities to your stay
4. Complete your personal information
5. Proceed to payment
6. Receive confirmation

### My Bookings
- View your current and past bookings
- Modify upcoming reservations
- Cancel bookings (subject to cancellation policy)
- Add reviews for completed stays

### User Profile
- Update your personal information
- Change your password
- View your booking history
- Manage your preferences

## Special Features

### AI Chatbot
Our virtual assistant can help you with:
- Answering questions about our resort
- Providing information about wildlife and activities
- Assisting with booking queries
- Troubleshooting common issues

### WhatsApp Support
Need immediate assistance? Click the WhatsApp button in the bottom corner to connect directly with our customer service team.

## Safari Guidelines

For the best safari experience:
- Wear neutral-colored clothing
- Bring sunscreen and a hat
- Follow all guide instructions
- Maintain a respectful distance from wildlife
- Keep noise levels to a minimum

## Booking Policies

- **Reservations**: Bookings require a 20% deposit
- **Cancellation**: Full refund if cancelled 48 hours before check-in
- **Children**: Special rates available for children under 12
- **Groups**: Discounts available for groups of 6 or more

## Need Help?

- Use the AI chatbot for immediate assistance
- Contact us via WhatsApp for urgent matters
- Visit our Contact page for email and phone details
- Check our FAQ section for common questions
